## Project Background
This project looks at a Nigeria fashion retail Business that sells clothing, footwear, and accessories through its physical store, website, instagram, and Whatsapp, with orders from Lagos, Abuja, Oyo, Rivers and Delta
This Data holds three years of sales data (2024 to 2026). This project brings it together in an interactive Excel dashboard to show what drives revenue and profit, and where the business can grow.
#### Insight and recommendations are provided on the following key areas:
### Sales Trends Analysis
### Product Performance 
### Regional Performance
### Campaign Impact
### Customer Behaviour

  ## Executive Summary
  ### Overview And Findings
  Across 2024 to 2026 the business earned 80.30 million in revenue and 31.37 million in profit from 2,527 units sold. Growth is real but slow. Revenue rose from 24.2M
  to 25.6M to 30.6M. In 2026 January started exceptionally high at approximately 3.50M, representing the high signal monthly spike across all the three years, although
  it dipped slightly in February, it rebounded strongly with another major peak in March
  
  <img width="900" height="457" alt="Sales Trends" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/178c5b02-da89-45d4-ae7b-ac6a2ac712ba" />

  Across all three years there is a consistent, sharp downward trend hitting a low point around June. For instance 2024 and 2026 both experience a dip heading into
  mid-year months, indicating a recurring seasonal slow period. May historically serves as a major performance high, and from August through December, the 2026 trend
  stabilizes into a steady band between 2M and 2.50M, showing more consistency compared to the wild fluctuations seen in previous years.

  Lagos and Abuja carry the business, together they bring in 56% of revenue, Lagos is the biggest market and Delta is the weakest.

  The top product changes every year, sneakers led in 2024, dresses in 2025, and handbags in 2026. Total across years clothing leads with 53% of total revenue.
  Sneakers, Dress, Jeans and Handbag are top earners. Cap, Belt are the weakest.

  Campaigns are not the engines that drive the business, most sales happen outside promotions. Regular, non-campaign sales make up roughly 70% of the revenue.

  <img width="559" height="429" alt="Sales Channel" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dfa6c116-46ab-4c09-9e88-29a68a658477" />

  Channels are uneven and customers are loyal. The best channel makes 26.6M against 14.7M for the weakest, almost double. Returning customers are steady at 54-57%
  each year.
